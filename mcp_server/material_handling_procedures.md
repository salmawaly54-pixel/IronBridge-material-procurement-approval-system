# Material Handling Procedures

**Policy ID:** 1
**Applicable Department:** Warehouse
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** IronBridge Construction — Warehouse & Safety Office

## Purpose

This policy defines safe lifting, storage, and transport procedures for
construction materials moving through IronBridge warehouses, including
the required personal protective equipment (PPE) and load limits per
material category. It exists so that anyone — human or assistant —
reasoning about a material reservation or dispatch understands the
physical handling constraints behind the numbers in the inventory
system, not just the numbers themselves.

## PPE Requirements by Category

| Category    | Required PPE                                             |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Cement      | Dust mask (P2/N95 minimum), gloves, safety glasses         |
| Steel       | Steel-toe boots, cut-resistant gloves, hard hat            |
| Plumbing    | Gloves, safety glasses (for PVC cutting/solvent use)        |
| Electrical  | Insulated gloves, dielectric footwear where live work applies |
| Concrete    | Steel-toe boots, gloves, back-support belt for manual lifts |

## Load Limits

- **Manual lifting:** no single employee may manually lift more than
  25 kg unassisted. Loads between 25–50 kg require a two-person lift or
  mechanical aid (pallet jack, forklift). Loads above 50 kg require
  mechanical handling equipment — manual lifting is prohibited outright.
- **Palletized loads:** must not exceed the rated capacity of the pallet
  or the lifting equipment used to move it, whichever is lower.
- **Steel stock (rebar, structural sections):** must be banded or
  strapped during transport; loose bundling is not permitted on-site.

## Transport Procedures

1. Materials leaving a warehouse for a project site must be logged
   against the originating Purchase Request before leaving the dock.
2. Hazardous or fragile materials (electrical cable reels, glass,
   solvent-based products) must be transported upright and secured
   against shifting.
3. Any material transport exceeding the vehicle's rated payload is
   prohibited regardless of urgency — split the load instead.

## Relationship to Purchase Requests

Material Handling Procedures apply from the moment a request is
**Reserved** through delivery at the site. They do not govern whether a
reservation should be approved in the first place — that is a budget
and stock-level question, covered by the Warehouse Safety Regulations
policy. This document only governs *how* the material physically moves
once a reservation exists.

## Non-Compliance

Any handling incident (dropped load, PPE violation, transport overload)
must be logged in the incident register and reported to the Warehouse
Supervisor on shift before the affected material is dispatched further.
